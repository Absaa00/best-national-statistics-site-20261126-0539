Five facts by region

Java

55.9% of people, 6.6% of land. Six provinces, including the capital Jakarta.

Sumatra

21.7% of people. Largest island economy after Java; palm oil and coal heartland.

Kalimantan

6.1% of people on 28% of the land — home of the new capital, Nusantara.

Sulawesi

7.4% of people. Nickel processing is reshaping its economy.

Maluku & Papua

3.9% of people on 25% of the land — the lowest densities in the country.
