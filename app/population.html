Population

281.6 million people in 2024, spread across 38 provinces and more than 17,000 islands. The median age is 30.3 years; 68.9% of people are of working age.

Age structure, 2024 (% of population)

The largest cohorts are 10–19 years old — a young, working-age nation.

Population by province

Java holds 55.9% of the population on 6.6% of the land.
