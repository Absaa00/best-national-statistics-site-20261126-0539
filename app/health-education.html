Health & Education

Life expectancy reached 73.9 years in 2024. Literacy among people aged 15 and over is 96.5%, and mean years of schooling is 9.2 years.

Life expectancy at birth (years)

Up from 71.9 years in 2020 to 73.9 in 2024.

Health system, 2024

Hospitals: 3,168 · Community health centres (puskesmas): 10,416 · National health insurance (JKN) members: 271 million, about 96% of the population.

Infant mortality: 15.9 per 1,000 live births. Stunting among children under five: 19.8%.

Schooling, 2024

Mean years of schooling: 9.2 · Expected years: 13.2 · Adult literacy (15+): 96.5% · Net enrolment, primary: 97.8% · junior secondary: 81.4% · senior secondary: 65.9%.

Students & teachers

About 53 million students from primary to senior secondary, taught by 3.4 million teachers. Higher education: 4,400 institutions, 9.3 million students.
